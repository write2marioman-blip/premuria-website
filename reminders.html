REMINDERS

Gentle prompts, not noise.

Reminders are there to reduce uncertainty and help the right person act at the right time.

Clear timing +

Reminders are linked to the medicine schedule.

A reminder appears around the time that matters, so nobody has to keep checking the clock. The aim is a useful prompt at the right moment, not a stream of alerts.

Care Circle awareness +

Shared care means the family can stay aligned rather than sending repeated messages.

When something is confirmed, the shared picture changes with it. That helps the next person know what has already happened without asking the same question again.

Your choice +

Members can choose whether they want notifications on or off.

Not everyone needs every prompt. Each person can keep reminders useful for their role, rather than turning Premuria into another source of unnecessary noise.

Simple language +

Prompts are written to be calm, direct and easy to understand.

A notification should tell you what matters and what to do next. Short wording keeps the message clear, especially when someone is busy or helping from a distance.

Private by design. Calm by default.
