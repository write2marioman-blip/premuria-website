RESPONSIBILITY

Everyone can see who is on duty.

A simple shared responsibility view helps care handovers stay clear.

Everyone in the Care Circle can see who is taking responsibility today. When plans change, responsibility can be handed over in seconds, so the whole family stays on the same page without calls, messages or guesswork.

One visible answer+

The Circle can see who is responsible today.

Everyone in the Care Circle can immediately see who is taking responsibility today. It removes the need to ask in a group chat, call around or assume somebody else is handling it.

Less duplication+

Two people do not need to assume the other person is handling it.

When responsibility is visible, two people are less likely to do the same task, and an important task is less likely to be missed because each person thought the other was doing it.

Simple handover+

Responsibility can be changed when family arrangements change.

Plans change. A handover should take seconds: the Care Circle can see the new responsibility clearly without rebuilding the day through messages and calls.

Shared context+

Responsibility sits alongside the same medicines, confirmations and notes.

Who is on duty is not isolated information. It sits beside the same medicines, confirmations and notes, giving everyone the context needed to understand the day.
