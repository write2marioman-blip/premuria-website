PRIVATE BY DESIGN

We don't need to know who Mum is.

Premuria starts with a simple rule: if we don't need it, we don't ask for it.

Premuria is designed around care coordination, not identity. The people in a Care Circle only need enough information to understand the routine and help each other stay organised.

That means keeping personal data to a minimum. The app does not need a full name, date of birth, home address or GPS location simply to show a medicine, a task or a confirmation.

Privacy is part of the product design, not an extra setting. We aim to collect only information that has a clear purpose inside Premuria and avoid data that does not help the family coordinate care.

Premuria does not need to know where Mum is.

No GPS tracking. No location history. No address is needed for the core care-coordination experience. Medicines, confirmations, notes and responsibility do not require us to know where the person is.

No profiling for advertising +

We do not use your activity to show you ads.

We do not use your activity to show you ads. Premuria is not built around advertising profiles or turning care activity into advertising data.

Only useful information +

We design every feature around data minimisation.

Premuria does not require Mum’s full name, date of birth, address, phone number, email address, diagnosis or location. We ask a simple question before adding data: does this genuinely help coordinate care?

Private by design. Calm by default.
